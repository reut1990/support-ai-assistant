### T-1073: Wrong shipping label address

> I bought 12 labels through Storely and they all printed with my old return address. The packages are already out. I want a refund for the labels.

Hi Noah,

Sorry about the labels printing with your old return address. Here's how to fix this for future labels:

1. Go to Settings > Locations and update your return address.
2. Then buy new labels from the order's page with Create shipping label.

Labels can be voided for a refund within 28 days, but only if they haven't been used. Since your packages are already out, I've passed your refund request to a specialist who will follow up with you.

Storely Support

### T-1067: Set up free shipping over $50

> How do I offer free shipping for orders over $50 but keep flat rate shipping below that?

Hi Diego,

Thanks for reaching out, and I can help you set up free shipping over $50.

1. Go to Settings > Shipping and delivery and open the zone that includes your customers' countries.
2. Click Add rate and add a flat rate for orders from $0 to $49.99.
3. Click Add rate again, name it Free shipping, and set the rate to $0.
4. Add the condition Order price from $50, then save.

Orders under $50 will pay your flat rate, and orders at $50 or more will get free shipping. If you have several zones, repeat these steps in each one.

Best,
Storely Support

### T-1074: Shipping profiles for heavy items

> Thanks for the help last week! Now I want furniture items to have a different shipping rate than small decor. How do I do that?

Hi Hannah,

Great to hear from you again! You can give furniture its own rate using a shipping profile:

1. Go to Settings > Shipping and delivery > Custom shipping rates.
2. Create a shipping profile for your heavy items.
3. Add your furniture products to that profile and set the rates you want for it.

If you have any trouble with these steps, just reply and we'll help further.

### T-1002: How do I download invoices?

> Where can I find my invoices for the last 6 months? My accountant needs them for tax filing.

Hi Diego,

Thanks for reaching out, and I can help you get your invoices for your accountant.

1. Go to Settings > Billing.
2. Under Bills, choose a date range covering the last 6 months.
3. Click Export to download a CSV of all your bills.

If you need to view a single invoice, click any bill instead. Past invoices can't be changed, so if your tax ID needs to appear on them, let us know and we can point you to the billing profile steps for future invoices.

Best,
Storely Support
