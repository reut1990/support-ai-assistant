### T-1073: Wrong shipping label address

> I bought 12 labels through Storely and they all printed with my old return address. The packages are already out. I want a refund for the labels.

Hello Noah,

Thank you for contacting Storely Support. You purchased 12 shipping labels through Storely, and they printed with your old return address after the packages had already gone out.

Labels take their return address from your location settings, so the old address was most likely still saved there when the labels were bought. Labels can be voided for a refund within 28 days, but only if they haven't been used.

To update your return address for future labels, please follow these steps:
1. Go to Settings > Locations and update your return address.
2. Check the address is correct before you buy any new labels.
3. Open the affected order and confirm the label status before your next shipment.

Refunds are only possible for unused labels within 28 days, so please reply if you need further help with the labels that have already been used.

For more details, please see our help article “Shipping rates, zones, local pickup and tracking”.

Kind regards,
Storely Support Team

### T-1063: Inventory not syncing with Amazon

> My Amazon integration app isn't syncing inventory. I oversold 4 items yesterday.

Hello Priya,

Thank you for contacting Storely Support. Your Amazon integration app is not syncing inventory, and four items were oversold yesterday.

Negative inventory appears when items are sold beyond the quantity in stock, and an external app can also update inventory on its own. We apologize for the inconvenience.

To stop further overselling, please follow these steps:
1. Open the affected product and turn on Track quantity.
2. Turn off Continue selling when out of stock.
3. Check the sync settings in your Amazon integration app, since it may be updating inventory.

If the inventory still does not sync after these steps, please reply to this message so we can review the issue further.

For more details, please see our help article “Manage orders, inventory and fraud risk”.

Kind regards,
Storely Support Team

### T-1067: Set up free shipping over $50

> How do I offer free shipping for orders over $50 but keep flat rate shipping below that?

Hello Diego,

Thank you for contacting Storely Support. You would like to offer free shipping on orders over $50 while keeping flat rate shipping for smaller orders.

You can do this with two rates in the same shipping zone, using a conditional rate for the free shipping threshold.

1. Go to Settings > Shipping and delivery and open the zone you want to edit.
2. Click Add rate and create a flat rate for orders from $0 to $49.99.
3. Click Add rate again, name it Free shipping, set the price to $0, and add the condition Order price from $50.

If the free shipping rate does not appear as expected at checkout, please reply to this message and our team can review your setup with you.

For more details, please see our help article “Shipping rates, zones, local pickup and tracking”.

Kind regards,
Storely Support Team

### T-1040: Guest checkout

> Some customers say they're forced to create an account. Can I allow checkout without an account?

Hello Priya,

Thank you for contacting Storely Support. You would like to know whether customers can check out without creating an account.

Yes, you can allow guest checkout by making customer accounts optional in your store settings.

1. Go to Settings > Checkout > Customer accounts.
2. Choose Accounts are optional.

Customers will then be able to complete checkout without creating an account. If customers continue to report a problem after this change, please let us know.

For more details, please see our help article “Discount codes and checkout problems”.

Kind regards,
Storely Support Team
