# What Storely offers: our products, services and plans

Storely is a platform for building and running your own online store. You get your store website, checkout, payments and the tools to manage orders, all in one place.

## What you can do with Storely
- **Build your store:** choose a free theme and customize it in **Online Store > Themes > Customize**, without writing code.
- **Sell your products:** add products one by one, or import up to 5,000 at a time from a CSV in **Products > Import**. Each product can have up to 100 variants, such as sizes and colors.
- **Take payments:** accept cards with **Storely Payments**, and get paid daily, weekly or monthly to your bank account. You can also use a third-party payment provider, which adds a transaction fee.
- **Ship orders:** set your own shipping rates and zones, offer free shipping over an amount you choose, or let customers pick up locally. Live carrier rates from carriers like UPS or USPS are available on Grow and Advanced.
- **Use your own domain:** connect a domain you already own. Every connected domain gets a free SSL certificate.
- **Run promotions:** create discount codes for your checkout.
- **Collect sales tax:** Storely calculates sales tax at checkout for the regions you set up.
- **Work with a team:** invite staff and choose exactly what each person can access.
- **Add more features:** install apps from the **Storely App Store**.

## Plans
| Plan | Monthly price | Staff accounts | Third-party payment fee |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Basic | $39 | 2 | 2.0% |
| Grow | $105 | 5 | 1.0% |
| Advanced | $399 | 15 | 0.6% |

Yearly billing saves 25%. All plans include your online store, Storely Payments, discount codes, shipping and tax tools, and a free SSL certificate. Grow and Advanced add live carrier shipping rates and more staff accounts. You can change plans at any time in **Settings > Plan > Change plan**.

## Where to start
1. Pick and customize a theme in **Online Store > Themes**.
2. Add your products in **Products**.
3. Set up payments in **Settings > Payments**, and shipping in **Settings > Shipping and delivery**.
4. Connect your domain in **Settings > Domains**.
