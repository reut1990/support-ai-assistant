# Shipping rates, zones, local pickup and tracking

## Shipping zones
Go to **Settings > Shipping and delivery**. A **zone** is a group of countries or regions. Customers can only check out to addresses inside one of your zones. To ship only to the US and Canada, make sure they're the only countries in your zones.

## Add rates
In a zone, click **Add rate** and choose:
- **Flat rate:** a fixed price.
- **Conditional rate:** based on order price or weight. Use this for free shipping above a certain amount.
- **Carrier-calculated rate:** live rates from carriers like UPS or USPS. Available on Grow and Advanced.

### Free shipping over $50
1. Add a flat rate for orders from $0 to $49.99.
2. Add a second rate called **Free shipping** at $0, with the condition **Order price** from $50.

## Shipping shows $0 for some customers
A zone has a rate set to $0 with no conditions. Check the zones for those countries, especially **Rest of world**.

## Different rates for different products
Create a **shipping profile** for heavy or special items in **Settings > Shipping and delivery > Custom shipping rates**, and add those products to it.

## Local pickup
Turn on local pickup for a location in **Settings > Shipping and delivery > Local pickup**. It only shows at checkout if the products are stocked at that location and the customer's address is in a country you deliver to.

## Shipping labels
Buy labels from an order's page with **Create shipping label** (US and Canada). Your return address on labels comes from **Settings > Locations**; update it there before buying labels. Labels can be voided for a refund within 28 days if they haven't been used.

## Tracking emails
Customers get a shipping confirmation email when you fulfill an order **with a tracking number**. Check that **Shipping confirmation** is turned on in **Settings > Notifications**. Orders fulfilled without tracking don't send the tracking link.

## International shipping
Labels bought through Storely for international orders include customs information from each product's **customs details**. Fill those in on each product page.
