# Understanding your bill, invoices and unexpected charges

Your Storely bill has three parts: your **plan subscription**, **app charges** from apps you've installed, and **transaction fees** if you use a payment provider other than Storely Payments.

## Download invoices
1. Go to **Settings > Billing**.
2. Under **Bills**, choose a date range.
3. Click any bill to view it, or **Export** to download a CSV of all bills.

To add a VAT or tax number to future invoices, go to **Settings > Billing > Billing profile** and enter it under **Tax ID**. Past invoices can't be changed.

## Charged twice?
A second charge is usually one of these:
- **Two stores** on the same card. Check **Settings > Billing** in each store.
- **A plan change mid-cycle.** Upgrading creates a prorated charge for the rest of the month.
- **A pending authorization** from your bank that disappears within 5 business days.

If none of these apply, contact Billing Support with the charge dates and amounts. Confirmed duplicate charges are refunded in full.

## App charges after uninstalling
Apps bill in 30-day cycles. When you uninstall an app, you aren't charged for future cycles, but charges for the current cycle may still appear on your next bill. If an app keeps charging you after a full cycle, contact the app developer first, then Billing Support.

## Billing currency
Subscriptions are billed in USD, EUR, GBP, CAD or AUD. To change currency, contact Billing Support. The change applies from your next billing cycle.
