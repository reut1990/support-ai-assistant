# Install, remove and troubleshoot apps

Apps from the Storely App Store add features to your store. Most apps are built by independent developers, who provide support for their own apps.

## Something broke after installing an app
1. Disable or uninstall the app you installed most recently.
2. Check your store again.
3. If that fixes it, contact the app developer from the app's page in **Apps**.

Two apps that edit the same part of your store, such as product pages or menus, can conflict with each other.

## An uninstalled app still shows on my store
Some apps add code directly to your theme. Uninstalling the app doesn't always remove that code.
- Check the app's uninstall instructions in its listing.
- Ask the app developer to remove leftover code.
- Or publish a fresh copy of your theme from the **Theme library**.

## An app changed or deleted my data
Storely can't undo changes an app makes to your products. Contact the app developer first, because many bulk editing apps keep a backup. To protect yourself, export your products to CSV from **Products > Export** before using bulk editing apps.

## Apps and store speed
Every app that adds scripts to your storefront makes it load a little slower. Remove apps you don't use.

## API access for developers
Store owners can create a **custom app** to get API credentials:
1. Go to **Settings > Apps > Develop apps**.
2. Click **Create an app** and choose which data it can access.
3. Click **Install** to generate the API access token. It's shown only once, so store it safely.

Developer documentation is at developers.storely.example.
