# Identity verification and Storely Payments account reviews

## Verification keeps failing
Verification fails most often because of:
- A name that doesn't exactly match your ID, including middle names.
- A photo with glare, cropped corners or a blurry date of birth.
- An expired document.
- A home address that doesn't match public records.

Take a new photo in good light with all four corners visible, and check that your name and date of birth in **Settings > Payments** match your ID exactly. After 3 failed attempts, the upload is locked for 24 hours. If it still fails after that, contact Payments Support to verify manually.

## Account deactivated or restricted
Storely Payments may restrict or deactivate an account when a review finds a high risk of fraud, chargebacks, or products that our payment partners don't allow. You'll receive an email with the reason.

If you believe the decision is wrong:
1. Reply to the email or contact Payments Support.
2. Include your website, what you sell, your fulfillment times, and supplier invoices if you have them.
3. While the review is open, add a third-party payment provider in **Settings > Payments** so you can keep taking orders.

Reviews usually take 2 to 5 business days. Orders that were already authorized but not captured stay valid for 7 days.

## Accept Apple Pay and Google Pay
Both are included with Storely Payments. Go to **Settings > Payments > Storely Payments > Manage** and turn on **Wallets**. They only appear on devices and browsers that support them.

## Multiple currencies
Turn on **Markets** in **Settings > Markets** to show prices and accept payments in local currencies. Payouts are still made in your store currency, after conversion.
