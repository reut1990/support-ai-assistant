# Change, pause or cancel your plan

## Plans
| Plan | Monthly price | Staff accounts | Third-party payment fee |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Basic | $39 | 2 | 2.0% |
| Grow | $105 | 5 | 1.0% |
| Advanced | $399 | 15 | 0.6% |

Yearly billing saves 25% on Basic, Grow and Advanced.

## Upgrade or downgrade
Go to **Settings > Plan > Change plan**. Upgrades take effect right away with a prorated charge. Downgrades take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.

When you downgrade, you keep your products, orders and customers. Features not included in the new plan, such as advanced reports or extra staff accounts, are turned off. Staff accounts above the new limit are deactivated, starting with the most recently added.

## Pause your store
If you're on Basic or higher for at least 60 days, you can switch to the **Pause and Build** plan for $9 a month. Your store stays online so customers can browse, but checkout is turned off. You can unpause at any time.

## Cancel
Go to **Settings > Plan > Deactivate store**. Your store closes at the end of the current billing cycle and you won't be charged again.

Storely doesn't refund unused time in a billing cycle, except for confirmed duplicate charges or billing errors. Before you deactivate, export your products, customers and orders from their pages in the admin.

## Store frozen after a failed payment
If a payment fails, your store is frozen after 7 days and checkout stops working. Update your card in **Settings > Billing > Payment method**, then click **Pay outstanding balance**. The store unfreezes within a few minutes of a successful payment. Updating the card alone doesn't retry the payment.
