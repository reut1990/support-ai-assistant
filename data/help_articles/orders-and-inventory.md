# Manage orders, inventory and fraud risk

## Edit an order
Open the order and click **Edit**. You can add or remove items, change quantities and swap variants, such as a size. If the new total is higher, Storely sends the customer an invoice for the difference. If it's lower, you can refund the difference.

## Cancel a duplicate order
Open the duplicate order, click **More actions > Cancel order**, and choose **Refund payment**. Restock the items if you won't ship them.

## Inventory showing negative numbers
Negative inventory means you sold more than you had. To stop it:
1. Open the product and turn on **Track quantity**.
2. Turn off **Continue selling when out of stock**.

If you sell on other channels through an app, the app may be updating inventory too. Check its sync settings.

## Bulk import products
Go to **Products > Import** and upload a CSV using our template. You can import up to 5,000 products at a time. Download the template from the import window.

## Variant limits
Each product can have up to **3 options** and **100 variants**. A product with 12 colors and 9 sizes (108 variants) is over the limit. Split it into separate products, for example one per color group.

## Fraud risk
Every order gets a **fraud analysis**: low, medium or high risk. For high-risk orders:
- Check that the billing and shipping addresses match.
- Look at the indicators listed in the order's fraud analysis.
- Contact the customer to confirm the order if you're unsure.

If you think the order is fraudulent, cancel and refund it before shipping. You can't be protected from a chargeback after you ship a high-risk order.

## Order notifications
Order emails go to the address in **Settings > Notifications > Staff order notifications**. Check the recipient list there, and add `notifications@storely.example` to your contacts.

## Draft orders
Create an order for a customer by phone in **Orders > Create order**. Click **Send invoice** to email them a payment link.

## Products missing from your store
Check the **Products** page filters, then **Settings > Activity log** to see who changed what. Products can be hidden by being set to **Draft** or removed from the **Online Store** sales channel. If the products are gone from the admin and the activity log shows no changes by you or your staff, contact Technical Support urgently.
