# Payout schedules, delays and bank accounts

## How payouts work
With Storely Payments, sales are grouped into payouts and sent to your bank account. Funds usually arrive **2 to 3 business days** after a payout is marked **Paid**, depending on your bank. Weekends and bank holidays add time.

## Change your payout schedule
Go to **Settings > Payments > Storely Payments > Manage > Payout schedule**. You can choose daily, weekly or monthly payouts. New stores have a 7-day waiting period before their first payout.

## Payout marked Paid but not in your bank
Wait 3 full business days after the **Paid** date. Then check that the bank details in **Settings > Payments** are correct. If the money still hasn't arrived, contact Payments Support with the payout ID and your bank's confirmation that nothing was received. We'll trace it with our banking partner.

## Payouts on hold
Payouts can be put on hold when:
- Identity or business verification is incomplete.
- Your bank account was rejected by the bank.
- There's an unusual increase in sales, refunds or chargebacks that needs review.

You'll see a banner in **Finances > Payouts** that explains what's needed. Most reviews finish within 3 business days after you provide the requested information. For holds longer than 5 business days without an explanation, contact Payments Support. This is treated as a priority request.

## Change your bank account
Go to **Settings > Payments > Storely Payments > Manage > Bank account** and click **Change**. The change applies to the next payout that hasn't started processing. A payout already in progress goes to the old account.
