# Edit your theme, fix display problems and speed up your store

## Edit your theme
Go to **Online Store > Themes > Customize**. From the editor you can change fonts and colors in **Theme settings**, edit the footer text in the **Footer** section, and add, remove or reorder homepage sections.

## Restore a previous version after an update
Theme updates install as a **new copy** of your theme in your theme library. Your previous version is kept.
1. Go to **Online Store > Themes**.
2. Find your previous theme in the **Theme library**.
3. Click **Actions > Publish**.

Customizations you made in the code editor aren't copied to the updated version. Make code changes in a duplicate of your theme, and keep a backup.

## Blurry images
Upload product images at **2048 x 2048 pixels**, in JPG or PNG. Images smaller than the section they're shown in are stretched and look blurry. Use the same aspect ratio for all product images.

## Mobile layout problems
Preview mobile in the theme editor using the phone icon. If an element runs off the screen, check for custom code or apps that add elements to product pages. Our free themes are tested on current phones; if a free theme displays wrongly with no apps or custom code, contact Technical Support.

## Store speed
The most common causes of a slow store are:
- Too many apps adding scripts to every page. Remove apps you don't use.
- Large images. Keep images under 2 MB.
- Large homepage sliders and videos.

Check your speed score in **Online Store > Themes > View report**.

## Paid themes
Paid themes are supported by their developer. Refunds for paid themes are available within 14 days of purchase if the theme hasn't been published. Contact the theme developer through the **Theme Store** page for help with features.
