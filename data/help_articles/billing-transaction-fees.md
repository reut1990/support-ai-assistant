# Transaction fees for third-party payment providers

If you accept payments through **Storely Payments**, you pay only the card processing rate. There's no extra Storely transaction fee.

If you use a **third-party provider** such as PayPal or Stripe as your main payment method, Storely charges a transaction fee on each order:

| Plan | Fee per order |
| --- | --- |
| Basic | 2.0% |
| Grow | 1.0% |
| Advanced | 0.6% |

This fee is billed on your Storely bill, separately from any fees your payment provider charges you.

## Offering PayPal without the fee
You can offer PayPal as an **additional** payment method alongside Storely Payments. The fee only applies when a third-party provider is your **main** card processor.

## Where to see fees
- Storely transaction fees: **Settings > Billing > Bills > Transaction fees**.
- Storely Payments processing fees on each payout: **Finances > Payouts**, then click a payout.
