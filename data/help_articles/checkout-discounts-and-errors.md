# Discount codes and checkout problems

## Discount code says "not valid"
Check these settings on the discount in **Discounts**:
- **Active dates:** the start date isn't in the future, and the time zone matches your store's.
- **Minimum requirements:** the cart meets the minimum amount or quantity.
- **Applies to:** the products in the cart are included.
- **Customer eligibility:** it isn't limited to specific customers.
- **Usage limits:** the code hasn't reached its total use limit or one use per customer.
- **Combinations:** it isn't blocked by another discount already applied.

## Customers can't complete checkout
1. Check status.storely.example for a reported outage.
2. Place a test order yourself using **Settings > Payments > Test mode**.
3. If you recently installed or updated an app or theme, it may add code to the cart page. Try disabling it.
4. Check that your payment provider is active in **Settings > Payments**.

If test orders fail with an error and there's no reported outage, contact Technical Support urgently. Include the error message and a screenshot.

## Abandoned checkouts
See which customers left during checkout in **Orders > Abandoned checkouts**. The most common reasons are unexpected shipping costs and forced account creation. Turn on automatic recovery emails in **Settings > Notifications > Abandoned checkout**.

## Guest checkout
Go to **Settings > Checkout > Customer accounts** and choose **Accounts are optional**.

## Other checkout settings
- **Tips:** turn on **Show tipping options** in **Settings > Checkout**.
- **Order notes and gift messages:** turn on **Order notes** in **Settings > Checkout**.
- **Checkout language:** checkout uses your store's default language from **Settings > Languages**.
- **Express checkout buttons:** turn on dynamic checkout buttons in your theme editor's product page settings.

Storely doesn't support minimum order amounts without an app.
