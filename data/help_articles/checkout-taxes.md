# Set up sales tax collection

Storely calculates sales tax at checkout based on the regions where you've told us to collect it. Storely doesn't file or pay taxes for you.

## Set up tax collection
1. Go to **Settings > Taxes and duties**.
2. Choose your country, then click **Collect tax** for each state, province or region where you're registered.
3. Enter your tax registration number for that region.

## Tax shows as 0% at checkout
- **The region isn't set up.** Check it's listed under **Collecting** in **Settings > Taxes and duties**.
- **The product isn't taxable.** Open the product and check that **Charge tax on this product** is turned on. This is the most common cause.
- **The shipping address is outside your collection regions.**
- **A tax override** is set for that product or collection.

Changes apply to new checkouts right away. They don't change orders that have already been placed.

## Tax-exempt customers
Open the customer in **Customers** and turn off **Collect tax**, or add the specific exemption for their region.
