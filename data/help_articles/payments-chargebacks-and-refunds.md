# Chargebacks and refunds

## Refunds
Refund an order from **Orders**, open the order, and click **Refund**. Refunds go back to the customer's original payment method. Storely sends refunds immediately, but banks take **5 to 10 business days** to show them to the customer.

If a refund shows **Pending** for more than 10 business days, your Storely Payments balance may not have enough funds to cover it. Check **Finances > Payouts** for a negative balance. Contact Payments Support if the balance is positive and the refund is still pending.

Processing fees from the original sale aren't returned when you refund.

## Chargebacks
A chargeback happens when a customer asks their bank to reverse a payment. The disputed amount plus a $15 chargeback fee is held from your balance.

### Respond to a chargeback
1. Go to **Orders > Chargebacks** and open the dispute.
2. Click **Submit response** and add evidence, such as tracking with delivery confirmation, customer messages, and your refund policy.
3. Submit before the deadline shown on the dispute, usually **7 days**. Missing the deadline means you lose the dispute automatically.

The bank makes the final decision, which can take up to 75 days. If you win, the amount and the fee are returned to you.

## Tips to prevent chargebacks
- Use a clear store name on card statements in **Settings > Payments > Statement descriptor**.
- Ship with tracking and require a signature for high-value orders.
- Respond quickly when customers contact you about a problem.
