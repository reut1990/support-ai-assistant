# Account security, login problems and staff access

## If you think your account was hacked
Act right away:
1. Use **Forgot password** on the login page to reset your password, if you still can.
2. Contact Account Security Support immediately and say your account is compromised. If you can't log in, use the **Can't access your account?** form. These requests are handled first.
3. We'll lock the account, pause payouts, and restore access after verifying your identity.

## Two-step verification codes not arriving
- Check that your phone can receive texts from short codes.
- If you changed phone carriers or numbers, use one of your **backup codes**.
- No backup codes? Use **Can't access your account?** to verify your identity and reset two-step verification. This takes 1 to 3 business days.

## Locked out after resetting your password
After 5 failed login attempts, accounts lock for 30 minutes. Wait 30 minutes, then log in with the new password. Don't request another reset while you wait, because each new reset link cancels the previous one.

## Staff accounts
Go to **Settings > Users and permissions > Add staff**. Choose exactly what each person can access, such as **Orders** without **Finances** or **Reports**. Invitation links expire after 7 days. To resend one, open the staff member and click **Resend invite**.

## Transfer store ownership
Go to **Settings > Users and permissions > Transfer ownership**. The new owner must already be a staff member. They'll need to add their own billing details and complete payment verification before payouts can continue.

## Manage several stores
You can sign in to several stores with one Storely ID. Each store has its own plan and billing.

## Close your store and export data
Before deactivating, export your **customers** (including marketing consent), **orders** and **products** from their pages using **Export**. After deactivation, store data is kept for 90 days, then deleted.
